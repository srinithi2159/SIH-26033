## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
AgriLink AI: Intelligent Agricultural Marketplace

AgriLink AI is a technology-driven platform designed to establish a direct connection between farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), individual consumers, retailers, restaurants, and wholesale purchasers. It allows farmers to showcase their agricultural products, update product availability, determine selling prices, and receive purchase requests without depending on multiple intermediaries.

The platform incorporates AI-powered demand analysis to estimate future market requirements using previous sales records, seasonal variations, weather patterns, and consumer purchasing behaviour. Additionally, its intelligent logistics module identifies efficient delivery routes and coordinates shipments to minimize transportation expenses and delivery delays.

Key Features:

- Direct digital trading between farmers and different categories of buyers.
- Intelligent agricultural price suggestion mechanism.
- Live product availability and stock monitoring.
- Online payment and transaction management.
- Transportation coordination and delivery tracking.

The proposed platform aims to create a more transparent agricultural trading environment, improve farmers' earning opportunities, minimize additional costs for consumers, and strengthen the overall agricultural distribution network.

## Technical Approach
Frontend: React.js / Flutter, HTML, CSS, JavaScript.

Backend: Node.js, Express.js, Python (AI/ML).

Database: PostgreSQL / MongoDB.

Artificial Intelligence:
- Demand prediction using LSTM / Prophet.
- ML-based price recommendations.
- AI-driven route optimization.

Cloud & Deployment: AWS / Azure, Docker.
Payments & Maps: UPI integration, Google Maps API / OpenStreetMap.

Methodology
Farmers list their products, buyers place orders, and AI predicts demand and suggests prices. The logistics module plans efficient delivery routes, while digital payments enable secure transactions.

Workflow
Farmer/FPO → Product Listing → AI Demand Prediction → Price Recommendation → Buyer Order → Route Optimization → Digital Payment → Delivery → Feedback.

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility Analysis:
Growing smartphone and internet usage in rural India supports digital adoption. UPI enables easy transactions, while cloud technology reduces operational costs. Publicly available agricultural data can support AI-based predictions.

Potential Challenges:
Limited digital literacy among farmers.
Transportation difficulties in remote areas.
Inaccurate or insufficient market data.

Risk Mitigation Strategies:
Collaborate with local FPOs and logistics partners.
Offer basic digital training to farmers.
Regularly update AI models with market data.
